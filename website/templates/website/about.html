Why we built this

Texting should be simple.
Compliance shouldn't be an afterthought.

We started SparkText after watching businesses struggle with two problems at once: clunky bulk-SMS tools that treated every contact as a number, and compliance nightmares that got accounts shut down overnight. Something had to give.

In 2024 we set out to build a platform where two-way conversations were first-class, opt-out handling was fully automatic, and 10DLC registration was handled for you — not bolted on as an afterthought.

Today we serve small businesses, marketing agencies, and operations teams across the United States who need reliable, carrier-compliant SMS without a full-time compliance team.
