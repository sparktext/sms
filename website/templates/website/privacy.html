This Privacy Policy describes how SparkText LLC ("SparkText", "we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information in connection with our SMS platform and website. By using the Service, you agree to the practices described here.

1. Who We Are

SparkText LLC is the data controller for personal information processed through the Service.

2. Data We Collect

Account & Billing

  • Name, email address, job title, and company name on registration.
  • Payment details processed by our payment processor (we store only last 4 digits and expiry).

Contact Data You Upload

  • Names, phone numbers, and custom fields in the lists you import.
  • Opt-in source, consent timestamp, and opt-out events for each contact.
  • Tags and segments you create. You are the data controller for your contacts' data.

Message Logs

  • Message content, direction, timestamp, delivery status, and carrier response.
  • Opt-out keyword events (STOP, UNSUBSCRIBE, QUIT, etc.).

Usage & Technical Data

  • IP address, browser type, OS, and referring URL when you access our website.
  • Session cookies and first-party analytics (anonymized).

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service.
  • To process payments and send invoices.
  • To send transactional emails (account alerts, delivery notifications).
  • To respond to support requests and monitor for abuse or compliance violations.
  • To generate anonymized, aggregated usage statistics for internal analytics.

We do not use your contacts' personal information for our own marketing purposes.

4. Sharing & Disclosure

We share your information only in the following circumstances:

  • Carriers & SMS providers (Twilio, Telnyx): Message content and recipient numbers to deliver messages.
  • Payment processors (Stripe): Billing information to process payments.
  • Legal requirements: When required by law, subpoena, or court order.
  • Business transfers: In a merger, acquisition, or asset sale.
🔒

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. Period.

5. SMS Data & Mobile Privacy

💬

Our commitment regarding SMS data

Mobile opt-in data and phone numbers collected through SparkText — including any data your contacts share when opting in to receive your messages — are never sold, rented, or shared with third parties for marketing purposes.

This data is used solely to deliver the messages you direct us to send on your behalf and to maintain compliant opt-out records.

We store opt-in consent records (source, method, timestamp) and opt-out events for each contact. These records are available in your dashboard and retained for the life of your account plus 12 months.

When a contact sends STOP (or equivalent), we immediately add them to your opt-out list and no further marketing messages are sent. This cannot be overridden.

6. Data Retention

  • Account data: Retained for the life of your account plus 30 days after termination.
  • Message logs: Retained for 24 months from the sent date.
  • Opt-out records: Retained for 12 months after account termination.
  • Billing records: Retained for 7 years as required by US tax law.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by emailing info@sparktext.si. Requests processed within 30 days.

7. Security

We implement encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+), encryption at rest, access controls, and regular security reviews. To report a potential vulnerability, email info@sparktext.si.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or port your personal information, or object to certain processing. Email info@sparktext.si to exercise any right — we respond within 30 days.

9. Cookies

We use strictly necessary session cookies to keep you logged in and a single first-party anonymized analytics cookie. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking pixels.

10. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 13. If you believe we've inadvertently collected information from a child under 13, contact us at info@sparktext.si and we will delete it promptly.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated by email and by updating the "Last updated" date. Continued use of the Service after changes take effect constitutes acceptance.
